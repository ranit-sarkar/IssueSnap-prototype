# IssueSnap

**Snap it. Track it. See it fixed.**

IssueSnap is a concept for reporting civic problems (potholes, garbage,
broken streetlights and more) with a photo and location, tracking each
report step by step, and confirming when it is fixed.

Built by first-year BTech CSE students for Lean In Hacks and the
West Bengal Seva First Innovation Challenge.

## Live demo
 https://ranit-sarkar.github.io/IssueSnap-prototype/

## What the prototype shows
- Sign up and log in with a simulated OTP
- Report a problem: category, camera photo, GPS location, note
- Five-step tracker: Received, Sent to authority, Assigned, In progress, Resolved
- Office view to move a report forward, or decline a fake one
- Confirm the fix and earn reward points
- English, Hindi and Bengali

## Important
This is a concept prototype. The OTP, office and data are simulated.
Nothing is sent to any real authority and nothing is stored online.
